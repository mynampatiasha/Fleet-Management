## Common Issues & Solutions

### ❌ "Please enter your email"
**Problem:** Email field is empty  
**Solution:** Enter your email address

### ❌ "Enter a valid email address"
**Problem:** Email format is incorrect  
**Solution:** Check your email spelling (e.g., user@example.com)

### ❌ "No account found with this email address"
**Problem:** Email is not registered  
**Solution:** 
- Check email spelling
- Make sure you're using the correct email
- Register a new account if needed

### ❌ "Too many requests. Please try again later"
**Problem:** You've requested too many password resets  
**Solution:** Wait 5-10 minutes and try again

### ❌ Email not received
**Problem:** Email hasn't arrived  
**Solution:**
1. Wait 2-3 minutes (emails can be delayed)
2. Check your spam/junk folder
3. Check you entered the correct email
4. Try requesting again

### ❌ "The action code is invalid or expired"
**Problem:** Reset link has expired (links expire after 1 hour)  
**Solution:** 
1. Go back to login screen
2. Click "Forgot Password?" again
3. Request a new reset link

### ❌ "Passwords do not match"
**Problem:** New password and confirm password don't match  
**Solution:** Make sure both passwords are exactly the same

## Tips for a Strong Password

✅ **Use at least 8 characters** (minimum is 6)  
✅ **Mix uppercase and lowercase letters**  
✅ **Include numbers**  
✅ **Add special characters** (!, @, #, $, etc.)  
✅ **Don't use common words** (password, 123456, etc.)  
✅ **Don't use personal information** (name, birthday, etc.)  
✅ **Use a unique password** (don't reuse from other sites)  

### Good Password Examples:
- `Fleet@2025!Secure`
- `MyAbra#Fleet99`
- `Tr@nsport2025!`

### Bad Password Examples:
- `password` (too common)
- `123456` (too simple)
- `abcdef` (too simple)
- `myname` (personal info)

## Security Reminders

🔒 **Never share your password** with anyone  
🔒 **Abra Fleet will never ask** for your password via email  
🔒 **Reset links expire** after 1 hour for your security  
🔒 **If you didn't request a reset**, ignore the email  
🔒 **Contact support** if you see suspicious activity  

## Need Help?

If you're still having trouble resetting your password:

1. **Check your email spelling** - Make sure it's correct
2. **Check spam folder** - Email might be there
3. **Wait a few minutes** - Emails can be delayed
4. **Try again** - Request a new reset link
5. **Contact support** - If nothing works, contact Abra Fleet support
