# GPS Button Not Working? Use Search Instead! 🔍

## Quick Answer

**The GPS button is not working because your device cannot get a good GPS signal in your current location (Kasturi Nagar). This is normal when you're indoors or surrounded by tall buildings.**

**✅ SOLUTION: Use the SEARCH feature instead of the GPS button!**

---

## Why GPS Button Doesn't Work

Your device GPS is showing:
- **Wrong location**: California instead of Bangalore
- **Poor accuracy**: 92-213 meters (should be under 20m)
- **Reason**: You're likely indoors or surrounded by buildings that block GPS signals

**This is NOT a bug in the app** - it's a physical limitation of GPS technology. GPS signals cannot penetrate buildings well.

## ✅ How to Use SEARCH Instead (RECOMMENDED)

### Step 1: Open Location Picker
When creating a roster, tap on "Select Pickup Location" or "Select Drop Location"

### Step 2: Use the Search Bar
At the top of the screen, you'll see a search bar with a magnifying glass icon 🔍

### Step 3: Type Your Location
Type one of these search queries:

**For Kasturi Nagar:**
```
Kasturi Nagar Bangalore
Kasturi Nagar Main Road Bangalore
Kasturi Nagar 1st Cross Bangalore
```

**For Specific Places:**
```
[Your Street Name] Kasturi Nagar Bangalore
[Your Building Name] Kasturi Nagar Bangalore
[Nearby Landmark] Kasturi Nagar Bangalore
```

### Step 4: Select from Results
- The app will show you a list of matching locations
- Tap on the correct one
- The map will zoom to that location
- Tap "Confirm Location" at the bottom
